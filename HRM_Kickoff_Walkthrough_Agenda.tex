dropdown, the title placeholder changing to match, the digitized form
fields, and the Required Documents panel blocking submission until
mandatory items are attached. Submit to DG. & \texttt{hr.finance} \\
6--7 min & \textbf{DG endorsement.} Quick --- open the submission,
endorse it, hand back to HR to forward to PSC. & \texttt{dg.mfem} \\
7--8 min & \textbf{PSC intake.} Show it arriving at Submitted, then
routed into Manager Checklist Review --- point out it's auto-routed, no
manual ``which unit'' decision needed. & \texttt{p.mahe} \\
8--11 min & \textbf{HR Unit Manager.} Show the submission landing
\emph{only} in this queue (not VIPAM's, not ODU's), work through the
checklist, assign to the Principal. & \texttt{gshem} (Shem George) \\
11--13 min & \textbf{Principal → Secretary → Commission.} Move quickly

13--17 min & \textbf{The new part --- decision → task allocation.} Use a

automatically --- the CommissionTask appearing in the HR Unit Manager's

Principal or Senior. & \texttt{s.tari}/\texttt{admin}, \texttt{gshem} \\
17--19 min & \textbf{What they'll do next.} Two phases: Phase 1,
