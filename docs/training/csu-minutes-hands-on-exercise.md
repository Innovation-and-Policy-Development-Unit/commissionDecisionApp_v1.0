# Hands-on exercise — taking minutes in SCDMS

**For:** Manager CSU  **Time:** about 30 minutes for Part 1, 15 minutes for Part 2

You will take the minutes of a practice sitting, save the draft, come back later
to review it, and send it on. Part 2 can be a few hours after Part 1 or the next day. Everything in the practice sitting is fictional and
marked `[TRAINING]`.

**Practice sitting:** `TRAIN-CSU-MTG-001` — TRAINING SITTING — minute-taking practice, not real

---

## Part 1 — take the minutes (during the sitting)

**1. Open the sitting**

- Sign in to SCDMS.
- In the menu go to **Minutes & Agenda → Minute intake**.
- Choose `TRAIN-CSU-MTG-001`. You should see 8 agenda items.

**2. Record the decisions**

For each item, choose the outcome in the **Decision** list. You do not need to type
"Approved" or "Deferred". Use these for the practice:

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Matters arising — appraisal report | Noted |
| Disciplinary matter — Senior Clerk | Approved |
| Appointment of Director of Policy | Approved |
| Acting appointment — Chief Accountant | Approved |
| Confirmation — three Administration Officers | Deferred |
| Corporate Services Division restructure | Returned |
| Contract extension — IT Systems Adviser | Approved |
| OPSC end-of-year office closure dates | Approved |

Press **Save draft**.

**3. Add notes only where something was said**

Notes are optional. Add them on two items so you can see the difference:

- *Disciplinary matter* — **Discussion notes:** "Members noted the family emergency and
  agreed a warning is enough." **Decision notes:** "Written warning. Recover salary for
  days not worked." **Action officer:** Manager Compliance
- *Confirmation — three Administration Officers* — **Decision notes:** "Deferred to the
  next sitting for the third officer's attendance record."

**4. Let the AI draft the paragraphs**

- On one item press **Format with AI** and read the **Formatted preview**. The paragraph
  is drafted from the submission, plus your notes if you typed any.
- Then press **Format all items**.
- Check: does each paragraph describe the submission correctly? Does it show the
  decision you chose? Does it claim anything was said that you did not type?

**5. Create the draft minutes**

- Press **Apply to minutes**.
- Go to **Minutes & Agenda → Minutes** and open the draft for `TRAIN-CSU-MTG-001`.
- Type one line for the opening and one for the closing. Save.
- Sign out.

---

## Part 2 — review and send on (a few hours later, or the next day)

**6. Review your draft**

- Sign in and open the draft again from **Minutes & Agenda → Minutes**.
- Check that everything from Part 1 is still there.
- Correct one sentence and press **Save Draft**.

**7. Wait for Manager Compliance**

This sitting has a compliance item, so Manager Compliance takes the minutes for it
(see her part below). **Submit for Review** stays greyed out until she has pressed
**Confirm Compliance Items**. The blue box at the top of the draft shows whether she has.

**8. Submit for review**

- Press **Submit for Review**. The draft goes to the Secretary first, then to the Chairman.
- If either of them presses **Return with Comments**, the draft comes back to you with a
  red box saying what to change. Fix it, ask Manager Compliance to confirm again, and
  submit again.

**9. Circulate**

- Once the Secretary and then the Chairman have approved, press
  **Circulate to Commissioners**.

**Stop here.** Do not send for signature or upload signed minutes.

---

## Manager Compliance — your part (about 10 minutes)

You take the minutes for the compliance item only: *Disciplinary matter — Senior Clerk*.

1. Open **Minutes & Agenda → Minute intake** and choose `TRAIN-CSU-MTG-001`.
   You will see only the compliance item.
2. Choose **Approved** in the **Decision** list, add a short discussion note, press
   **Save draft**, then **Format with AI**.
3. Press **Apply to minutes**. Only your item in the draft changes.
4. In the draft (**Minutes & Agenda → Minutes**) you can read everything but edit only
   your item. Correct a word and press **Save Draft**.
5. Press **Confirm Compliance Items**. Manager CSU can now submit the draft.
