# Live outcomes exercise — Manager CSU records, Commissioners watch

**Who:** Manager CSU (records), Chairman and Commissioners (decide and watch their Sitting Pack).
**Sitting:** `TRAIN-MTG-099`. Live outcomes are switched on for it; they are off for every other sitting.

## Before the exercise

1. The agenda has been approved by the Secretary, endorsed by the Chairman and circulated (the live demo).
2. The Secretary or an Admin opens **Commission Sittings**, opens the training sitting and presses **Launch Operations**. Until then the outcome buttons stay hidden.
3. Commissioners open **Sitting Pack** and choose the training sitting. Manager CSU opens **Minute intake** for it.

## What is decided, from Minutes No. 23

The practice papers take the outcome of a comparable decision in the real No. 23 minutes:

| Practice paper | Outcome | Like No. 23 decision |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate Services restructure | Approved | `03(j)-23-2026` appointment |
| Director of Policy appointment | Approved | `20-23-2026` awards |
| Contract-staff request | Not approved | `62-23-2026` risk allowances |
| Acting appointment (deferred before) | Deferred | `03(o)-23-2026` / `16-23-2026` |
| Other Matters item a Commissioner raises | Noted | `03(l)-23-2026` |

## Steps

1. The Chairman takes the items in agenda order and asks for the Commission's decision. The Commissioners say it aloud, as in the table.
2. Manager CSU opens the item in Minute intake and presses the outcome button: **Approved**, **Not approved**, **Deferred** or **Noted**.
3. Within a few seconds the matching coloured label appears on that item in every Commissioner's Sitting Pack. Commissioners confirm they see it.
4. Press the same button again to clear it, or another to change it. Try one correction.
5. The decision line for the minutes is filled in for Manager CSU (only if it was empty), so the minutes start from what was decided.

## Remember

- The label shows what was announced in the room. The **minutes** are still the record, and tasks still come from the **signed** minutes.
- Only Manager CSU, the Secretary, the Senior Admin Officer and an Admin can record outcomes. Commissioners can only watch.
- To turn the feature on for another sitting, set `show_live_outcomes` on the meeting (Django admin). It is off by default.
