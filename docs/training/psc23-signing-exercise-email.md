PART 1: DOWNLOAD, SIGN AND UPLOAD (you)

1. Sign in and open Minutes & Agenda > Minutes. Open `TRAIN-PSC23-MTG-001`.
2. Read the minutes. Check that all 16 items are there, each with its decision and its Action Officer.
3. Press "Download for Signature". This is the copy that would be printed for signing. Open the PDF and check it reads properly.
4. For this exercise the attached, already signed Minutes No. 23 is your signed copy. Nothing needs printing.
5. Press "Upload Signed Minutes" and choose the attached PDF. Wait for the upload to finish.
6. The status changes to Signed. Press "View Signed Scan" and check the signed minutes open. Scroll to the pages with the decisions.
7. Try a correction: press "Re-upload Signed Minutes", choose the same file again and confirm. "View Signed Scan" should still open the file.

Nothing is sent to anyone in Part 1, and no tasks exist yet.

PART 2: HAND OVER FOR ALLOCATION (a Secretary)

Only a Secretary (or an Admin) can allocate. Please tell the Secretary when your upload is done.

8. The Secretary opens `TRAIN-PSC23-MTG-001`, presses "View Signed Scan" to confirm it is the right document, then presses "Allocate Decision Tasks to Managers". This can be done once only.
9. You: open Outcomes > Minutes decision tasks. You should see 16 tasks in the register. Four are yours (Manager Responsible: you): item 1 (adoption of the agenda), item 2 (confirmation of earlier minutes), item 3 (Director IPDU) and item 6 (Public Service Day preparations).

PART 3: ALLOCATE AND WORK YOUR OWN TASKS (you, then your officer)

10. Open your notification "Commission decision allocated to your unit", or open the task register. Open the task for item 3, the Director IPDU decision.
11. Read the Decision Detail. Is it usable as it came from the minutes?
12. Press Edit. Under "Assign staff", tick the officer who should act, set a Due date and save.
13. Do the same for items 1, 2 and 6. Choose a different officer for item 6.
14. Optional: on item 6 press "Add Subtask" and break the work into steps, for example confirming the Motalava venue and the Commission members' tickets.
15. The officer opens the task, sets Task Status to In progress, posts a short update, then sets it to Completed.
16. You: confirm the task shows the update and Completed.

PART 4: CHECK THE WHOLE PICTURE (you)

17. In the task register, check the other 12 tasks went to the right managers:
