Payment Terms and Conditions
  1. Cost Recovery: The Special Skills Allowance is to be funded by {{ funding_source|default:"the requesting organization" }}. The Commission notes your confirmation that the cost will be met from {{ funding_ministry|default:"organizational" }} resources.
  2. Time-Bound: The allowance is finite and time-bound to the period specified above. It does not create an ongoing financial commitment beyond {{ allowance_end_date|date:"d F Y" }}.
  3. Payment Schedule: The allowance shall be processed through the regular payroll system for {{ allowance_frequency|default:"monthly" }} payment, commencing from {{ allowance_start_date|date:"d F Y" }}.
  4. Taxation: The allowance is subject to all applicable tax and statutory deductions in accordance with Vanuatu tax law.
  5. Policy Compliance: The approval is consistent with the Public Service Commission Act [CAP 246] and the Public Service Staff Manual (PSSM), particularly Chapter 4, Section 4.2.10 governing Special Skills Allowances.
  6. Cessation: The allowance will automatically cease on {{ allowance_end_date|date:"d F Y" }} unless formally extended by further PSC decision.
Value for Money

The Commission notes that the approved allowance represents significant value for money to Government. The equivalent cost of engaging an external consultant to undertake comparable work would substantially exceed the approved allowance amount, demonstrating the cost-effectiveness of this arrangement.

Performance Expectations

The Commission trusts that you will continue to execute these specialized responsibilities with the same professionalism, diligence, and excellence that have characterized your distinguished tenure in the public service. The Commission places on record its confidence in your abilities and your commitment to achieving the organizational objectives outlined in your assignment.

We wish to acknowledge your exceptional contribution to the public service and thank you for your dedication and commitment to advancing the Government's strategic priorities.
