# Commissioner Training Program — SCDMS

**Audience:** The Chairperson and Commissioners of the Public Service Commission (roles `chairperson`, `psc_commissioner`)
**Delivered by:** OPSC Secretariat with IPDU (system support)
**Format:** One 2-hour in-person session + a 30-minute follow-up at the first live sitting
**Target window:** Week of 9 November 2026 (tentative — follows HR training, then OPSC staff training; the Commission has not confirmed dates)

---

## 1. Why this training exists

Under the pilot, every ministry lodges through SCDMS. Commissioners will therefore receive agendas, supporting papers and minutes digitally instead of on USB flash drives and paper. The aim is that each Commissioner can, without help:

1. Connect to SCDMS securely from home or the sitting venue.
2. Read the agenda and every supporting paper *before* a sitting.
3. Keep private preparation notes that no one else can see.
4. Review and comment on circulated minutes within the two-working-day window.
5. Know what is still done on paper (wet-ink signature on minutes) and what is not.

Commissioners are not asked to learn the submission workflow, checklists, or unit assessment — those belong to HR, OPSC and unit staff.

## 4. Modules

### Module 1 — Getting in

**Learning outcome:** Connect through the VPN and sign in without assistance.

- SCDMS is on the government private network only. It is reached through the DCDT VPN (HTTPS only); there is no public website.
- Connect the VPN first, then open `https://scdms.psc.gov.vu`.
- Change your temporary password at first sign-in (**Account** page). Use a password you do not use anywhere else.
- Enable two-factor sign-in if offered on your account.
- Never share VPN or SCDMS credentials. DCDT logs all VPN access, and every action in SCDMS is attributed to your account.
- Sign out when finished on any shared or borrowed device.

**Practice:** Each Commissioner connects the VPN, signs in, changes their password, and opens the Dashboard.

### Module 2 — Reading the agenda and papers

**Learning outcome:** Find the agenda for the next sitting and open every supporting document, before the meeting.

- When the Chairperson endorses the agenda, you receive an email with an index of items (titles and references only) and an in-app notification. **The email is not the papers** — sign in to read them.
- Open **Commission Decision → Minutes & Agenda → Agenda** and select the sitting.
- Agenda items are grouped by section (e.g. structure/organisation, appointments). Open an item to see the submission summary, the AI executive brief and quality score (advisory only), and the attached documents.
- **Deferred agenda** lists items carried over from earlier sittings, with their history.
- Submission signatures: HR obtains the Director-General's approval on paper and uploads the signed copy. SCDMS records the DG's name and signing date and shows an advisory "signature seen / not found" hint. It does not prove a signature is genuine; raise any doubt with the Secretary.
- The AI brief is a reading aid. Decisions are based on the papers and the Commission's deliberation.

**Practice A:** Open the training sitting's agenda. For each of the four practice items: open the documents, read the brief, and say which section it sits under.

### Module 3 — Private preparation notes

**Learning outcome:** Prepare for a sitting using notes only you can see.

- Each submission has a **private note** per Commission member. No other Commissioner, the Secretary, or the Administrator can read yours.
- The **My notes** view lists every agenda item in order with its documents, so it doubles as a pre-meeting reading list. Items without a note appear empty so you can jump straight in.
- Private notes are separate from the shared comment features. Do not put anything in a private note that you would need the Secretariat to act on.

**Practice B:** Write a note on at least two dummy items. Then open My notes and confirm they appear in agenda order. Ask a neighbour to open the same item and confirm they cannot see your note.

### Module 4 — Other Matters and the sitting

**Learning outcome:** Raise an item under Other Matters and understand what the Secretariat does in the room.

- Commissioners and the Chairperson can add an item under **Other Matters** for a sitting.
- During the sitting the Secretariat may run the **Sitting Pack** on a tablet (watermarked, available during the live sitting only). It is not a take-home copy.
- Sittings may be recorded. The recording is used by the Secretariat to prepare minutes through AI-assisted transcription and review; the recording does not replace the signed minutes.
- Reading in advance (Module 2) is the expected norm — the sitting is not the time to open papers for the first time.

**Practice C:** Add an Other Matters item to the training sitting. The trainer confirms it appears on the agenda.

### Module 5 — Minutes: review, comment, sign

**Learning outcome:** Respond to circulated minutes in time and know how signing works.

The real-world sequence is:

1. The minute-taker (Senior Administration Officer) drafts the minutes.
2. The Secretary and Chairperson each approve within **1 working day**.
3. The Secretary **circulates the minutes to all Commissioners**. You receive a notification.
4. You have **2 working days** to comment. Open **Minutes**, select the sitting, and add your comments. Comments are visible to the Secretariat and other Commission members.
5. When feedback is in, the minutes return to the minute-taker.
6. The minutes are printed and **signed in wet ink** at the next sitting. SCDMS does not yet accept digital signatures for minutes (pending legislation).
7. The Secretariat uploads the scanned signed copy. The Secretary then separately releases the follow-up tasks to unit managers.

Your two responsibilities in SCDMS: comment on time, and sign the paper copy.

**Practice D:** Open the dummy circulated minutes. Add one comment. Find the comment deadline shown on the minutes.

### Module 6 — Security, getting help, Q&A

- Only open SCDMS over the VPN. Do not forward agenda emails or screenshots of papers outside the Commission.
- Treat any papers saved to a personal device as confidential; delete them when no longer needed.
- If you suspect someone else has used your account, tell the Secretary and IPDU immediately so the password can be reset.
- Support contacts and hours: *(Secretariat to insert before the session)*.
- Collect questions and "I got stuck" notes on a flipchart for the follow-up session.

## 5. Training sitting (practice data)

Build it with one command (Secretariat/IPDU, a day or two before the session):

```bash
docker exec scdms-backend-1 python manage.py seed_training_sitting
```

It creates a sitting named "TRAINING SITTING — practice only, not real" with an already-circulated agenda, so Commissioners start exactly where they would after the Chairperson endorses a real agenda:

| # | Item | Used for |
|---|------|----------|
| 1 | Fictional ministry restructure, with proposal letter and before/after chart | Opening documents, reading the brief |
| 2 | Fictional senior appointment, with signed DG approval letter | Seeing the paper DG approval |
| 3 | Fictional leave-policy amendment, 12-page PDF | Long document reading |
| 4 | Fictional acting appointment, deferred from a previous practice sitting | Deferred agenda view and its history |
| — | Other Matters left empty | Exercise C |

It also creates a completed previous sitting whose minutes are already circulated to Commissioners, with a two-day comment deadline and one example comment, for Exercise D.

The practice data is safe to load on the live system:

- Records use `TRAIN-...` reference numbers, so real PSC submission and meeting numbering is not used up.
- The AI briefs and quality scores are pre-written, so no AI calls are made now or when a Commissioner opens an item.
- No emails or notifications are sent, so Commissioners do not receive a real "agenda circulated" email.
- It uses a separate "Training Ministry" and touches no user accounts.

Remove all of it afterwards (this also deletes the Commissioners' practice notes on these items):

```bash
docker exec scdms-backend-1 python manage.py seed_training_sitting --cleanup
```

## 6. Assessment and sign-off

No test. A Commissioner is **signed off** when the trainer has seen them do each of these unaided:

| Skill | Seen |
|-------|------|
| Connect the VPN and sign in | ☐ |
| Change password | ☐ |
| Open the next sitting's agenda | ☐ |
| Open a supporting document from an agenda item | ☐ |
| Write a private note and find it again in My notes | ☐ |
| Add an Other Matters item | ☐ |
| Add a comment on circulated minutes | ☐ |

Anyone not signed off gets a 30-minute one-to-one before the first live sitting.

## 7. Quick-reference card (print one page per Commissioner)

**Getting in:** Connect VPN → `https://scdms.psc.gov.vu` → sign in.

**Before every sitting:**
1. You get an "agenda circulated" email. It is only an index.
2. Sign in → **Commission Decision → Minutes & Agenda → Agenda**.
3. Open each item and read the documents.
4. Write private notes (only you can see them).

**After a sitting:**
1. You get "minutes circulated" — comment within **2 working days** under **Minutes**.
2. Sign the printed minutes at the next sitting.

**Problems?** Contact: *(insert Secretariat and IPDU numbers).* Do not share your password with anyone, including support staff.
