# From the signed minutes to allocated decisions — exercise for Manager CSU

You will take the minutes of **PSC Meeting No. 23 of Friday 2 October 2026**
through the last stage of the Commission process in SCDMS: download them for
signing, upload the signed copy, then follow the decisions out to the units that
must act on them.

- **Sitting in SCDMS:** `TRAIN-PSC23-MTG-001` ("TRAINING SITTING — replica of PSC
  Meeting No. 23 …").
- **Minutes:** already written and reviewed, status **Awaiting signature**.
- **Test submissions:** `TRAIN-PSC23-001` to `016`, one for each of 16 real
  decisions from the minutes. They keep the real decision number (for example
  `03(n)-23-2026`) and the Action Officer named in the minutes. Names of officers,
  candidates and disciplined employees have been removed.
- **The signed minutes:** the file `PSC_Minutes_No_23_of_2_October_2026_signed.pdf`
  (96 pages) comes with this exercise. It is the real signed Minutes No. 23. You
  upload it as the signed copy.

Everything you create here is practice. The sitting and its papers are marked
`TRAIN-` / `[TRAINING]`.

## Part 1 — Download, sign and upload  (you, Manager CSU)

1. Sign in and open **Minutes & Agenda → Minutes**. Open `TRAIN-PSC23-MTG-001`.
2. Read the minutes. Check that all 16 items are there, each with its decision and
   its Action Officer.
3. Press **Download for Signature**. This is the copy that would be printed for the
   Chairman and Secretary to sign. Open the PDF and check it reads properly.
4. *Signing:* for this exercise the already signed Minutes No. 23 is your signed
   copy. Nothing needs printing.
5. Press **Upload Signed Minutes** and choose
   `PSC_Minutes_No_23_of_2_October_2026_signed.pdf`. Wait for the upload to finish.
6. The status changes to **Signed**. Press **View Signed Scan** and check the signed
   minutes open, page 1 first. Scroll to the decisions pages.
7. Try a correction: press **Re-upload Signed Minutes**, choose the same file again,
   and confirm. **View Signed Scan** should still open the file.
8. Sign out and try to open the signed scan from a private browser window without
   signing in. It should refuse.

*Nothing is sent to anyone in Part 1, and no tasks exist yet.*

## Part 2 — Hand over for allocation  (a Secretary)

Only a Secretary (or an Admin) can allocate. Tell the Secretary the signed scan is
uploaded and ready to check.

9. **Secretary:** open `TRAIN-PSC23-MTG-001`, press **View Signed Scan** and confirm
   it is the right document, then press **Allocate Decision Tasks to Managers**.
10. **You:** open **Outcomes → Minutes decision tasks**. You should now see 16 tasks
    in the register. Four are yours (Manager Responsible: you): items 1, 2, 3 and 6.

*This step notifies and emails each unit manager, tells Commission members the
signed minutes are on record, and queues draft outcome letters for the approved
papers. It can be done once only.*

## Part 3 — Allocate and work your own tasks  (you, then your officer)

11. Open your notification "Commission decision allocated to your unit", or open
    **Outcomes → Minutes decision tasks**. Open the task for item 3, the Director
    IPDU decision.
12. Read the **Decision Detail**. Is it usable as it came from the minutes?
13. Press **Edit**. Under **Assign staff**, tick the officer who should act (for
    example the Senior Officer for the advertisement), set a **Due date** and save.
14. Do the same for items 1, 2 and 6, choosing a different officer for item 6.
15. Optional: on item 6 press **Add Subtask** and break the work into steps (for
    example: confirm the Motalava venue; confirm Commission members' tickets).
16. **Officer:** open your task, set **Task Status** to **In progress**, post a
    short update, then set it to **Completed**.
17. **You:** confirm the task now shows the update and Completed.

## Part 4 — Check the whole picture  (you)

18. In **Outcomes → Minutes decision tasks**, check the other 12 tasks went to the
    right managers using the table above. Note any that went to the wrong place.
19. Open item 7 (the discipline report) and item 10 (deferred). Do the two decision
    types read differently?
20. Open the test submission `TRAIN-PSC23-009` (the vehicle damage notice). Does its
    journey show the decision and the allocation?

## What to note down

- Which screen was hard to find?
- Part 1: did **Download for Signature** produce something you would be happy to
  print and sign? Was the 96-page upload quick enough?
- Part 2: did every decision become a task for the right manager?
- Part 3: did the officer list show the right people, and only them?
- Did the notifications and emails arrive, and did their links open the right place?
- Where is the real process different from this one (for example: more than one
  Action Officer on a decision; decisions with no unit)?

## Not part of this exercise

- Writing and reviewing the minutes: that is the separate minute-taking practice
  (`TRAIN-CSU-MTG-001`).
- Sending outcome letters to ministries: drafts are queued for the practice papers.
  Do not send them.

## For the facilitator

Before Part 2, tell **Manager HR, Manager VIPAM, Manager Compliance, the Chairperson
and any Commissioners**: allocating sends them real notifications and emails, and
the officers they pick in Part 3 are emailed too. The signed Minutes No. 23 contains
disciplinary cases that name real employees: give the PDF only to the person doing
the exercise, and do not forward the email.

Set up (once, on the server):

```bash
sudo -n docker exec scdms-backend-1 python manage.py seed_psc23_signing_exercise
```

Remove everything afterwards, including the tasks, the practice papers and every
uploaded scan:

```bash
sudo -n docker exec scdms-backend-1 python manage.py seed_psc23_signing_exercise --cleanup
```

`/opt/scdms/local/training/PSC_Minutes_No_23_of_2_October_2026_signed.pdf`.
