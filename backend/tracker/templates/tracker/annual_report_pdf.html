8. Methodology

  • All figures are derived from the SCDMS workflow record; each decision and stage change is timestamped at the moment it was recorded.
  • Submissions received counts papers lodged with the Commission in the calendar year, excluding attachments to parent submissions.
  • Decisions recorded counts the first Commission decision (approved / rejected / returned) entered for each submission during the year.
  • Days to decision measures receipt by PSC to the recorded Commission decision, in calendar days.
  • Implemented within target uses each submission's explicit implementation due date where set, otherwise {{ d.implementation.target_days }} calendar days from Commission approval.
  • Formally served refers to outcome letters served through the system's decision-service record, each protected by a SHA-256 integrity hash.
  • Dataset frozen on {{ generated_on|date:"d F Y" }}; figures are reproducible from the stored snapshot.
